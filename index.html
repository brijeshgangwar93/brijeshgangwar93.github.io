About

Photonics, IIT Bombay — nanofabrication to nonlinear optics

I'm a Research Associate in the Department of Physics at IIT Bombay, working in the Laboratory of Optics of Quantum Materials (LOQM) under Prof. Anshuman Kumar. I completed my PhD here as a recipient of the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF). My work sits at the intersection of nanofabrication and nanophotonics — building optical structures small enough that the shape of the device changes the physics of the light inside it.

Before IIT Bombay, I completed an M.Tech at IIT Kharagpur and an M.Sc. at MJP Rohilkhand University, and qualified GATE Physics in 2018. That path took me from broad physics training into a focused interest in photonics and optics.

My current research centers on bound states in the continuum (BICs) in 2D van der Waals materials — a way of trapping light that, in the ideal case, never leaks out, with real potential to reshape how compact photonic devices are built. I also work on whispering-gallery-mode microresonators, where fabrication choices like focused-ion-beam polishing determine how close real devices get to their theoretical limits.

I'm an active member of Optica (formerly OSA) and the IEEE Photonics Society, and I help organize photonics and optics events through the OSA–IEEE Photonics Student Chapter at IIT Bombay. I also have experience as a Teaching Assistant across several physics courses.

Research

Two directions, one question: how far can confined light be pushed?
Direction A

Whispering-gallery modes in microresonators

Light circulating at the rim of a microdisk builds up intensity through total internal reflection, enabling strong nonlinear effects at low input power. Sidewall roughness is the main loss channel — my work uses focused-ion-beam polishing on SiO₂ microdisks (5–20 µm diameter) to push quality factors closer to their theoretical ceiling.

MicroresonatorsFIB fabricationNonlinear optics
Direction B

Bound states in the continuum in photonic metasurfaces

BICs are modes that coexist with a continuum of radiating states yet remain, in the ideal case, perfectly confined — giving metasurfaces with very high Q despite being open, planar structures. I work on realizing and probing these states, including in 2D-material and hBN-integrated platforms for sensing and single-photon emission.
